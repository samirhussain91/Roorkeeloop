💚 Hamara Motive

Roorkee me naye aane wale student, family ya kisi bhi visitor ko Hospital, Dawa, Tiffin, Bus, Train ke liye idhar-udhar bhatakna na pade. Isliye humne Roorkee ki saari zaroori jaankari ek jagah jama ki hai. Hamara motive sirf madad karna hai - sahi number, sahi address, sahi time pe.

RoorkeeLoop kisi se paise nahi leta. Ye website Roorkee ke logo ke liye pyaar se banayi gayi hai.

⚠️ Hamari Zimmedari / Responsibility

1. Hum koshish karte hai ki har number aur address sahi ho.
2. Agar koi number band ho ya badal gaya ho, toh hume bataye - hum turant update karenge.
3. Emergency me hamesha pehle 112 / 108 pe call karein.
4. Koi bhi dukaan / hospital humse paise deke upar nahi aa sakta. Sab barabar hai.
